## Institutional review and implementation — not respondent copy

The supplied proposal states that the Board manages recruitment, admissions and the Google account. That operating arrangement does not itself settle the controller's legal status.

Before publication:

1. Confirm controller identity/authority and contact/address/DPO details with AGH. If AGH is the controller, follow university guidance and obtain its DPO's confirmation of the basis. Article 6 limits reliance on legitimate interests by public authorities performing their tasks.
2. If independent society control is confirmed, approve and document the legitimate-interest, necessity and balancing assessment. The proposed basis is not yet established.
3. Document the account's actual Google service edition, provider roles, applicable terms/agreements, recipients and international transfer mechanisms/safeguards. Review [Google's terms](https://policies.google.com/terms), [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [transfer information](https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks). Google describes global processing and transfer frameworks; these public statements do not establish which contracts or safeguards apply to this account. No accepted processing agreement, EU-only storage or particular contractual safeguard has been verified here. Record the applicable arrangement and how applicants can obtain safeguard information before finalizing the notice.
4. Approve the proposed 90-day decision/withdrawal/admission deadlines, a separate membership-register retention policy and a deadline for unresolved applications. Assign a deletion owner and review the relevant decision/withdrawal/admission dates. Delete originals from both Forms and Sheets and handle exports/copies; transfer only necessary register data. No automated transfer/deletion process is implemented by this setup task.
5. Approve exact bilingual wording, version/date, restricted Board access and launch. Keep access lists, internal links, assessments, approval records and applicant data private. Remove all review markers before publication. Do not put this proposed text in `APPROVED_PRIVACY_NOTICE` or label it approved merely because the website is ready.
