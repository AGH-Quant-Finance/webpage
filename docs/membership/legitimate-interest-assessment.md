# Membership recruitment — proposed legitimate-interest assessment

**Draft assessment, 14 October 2026. Awaiting Board and institutional/legal review; not authorization to collect responses.** No applicant records or private account identifiers belong in this document.

## Scope and controller assumption

The society identifies **Koło Naukowe Quant Finance, operating at AGH University of Krakow**, contact **agh.quant.finance@gmail.com**, as controller. The Board reports independent responsibility for organizing recruitment, choosing collected information and managing applications. That supports examining its decision-making role, but does not settle its legal status or relationship with AGH. Confirm the arrangement before relying on it. If AGH controls processing, its guidance must determine the basis; Article 6(1)(f) is unavailable to public authorities when performing their public tasks. [GDPR Article 6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679).

Scope: review voluntary membership applications, contact candidates and make Board admission decisions. Project selection, marketing and ongoing register administration are separate purposes, not approved by this assessment.

## Purpose test

The proposed interest is administering voluntary student membership: identifying applicants, communicating decisions and understanding academic interests. Applicants seek a route into the community. This concrete organizational purpose does not justify extra employment-style evidence or ranking finance experience. Confirm authority under the society's regulations; membership remains a human Board decision.

Provisional conclusion: the purpose appears capable of being legitimate if the society is entitled to administer membership and processing remains within this scope.

## Necessity test — seven existing questions

| Data | Proposed necessity or alternative |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Identify candidates and distinguish applications; no identity-document upload is needed. |
| Email | Contact candidates and communicate decisions. An AGH address is preferred, not mandatory. |
| Field/year of study | Understand student context and assess applicable eligibility. Review whether both elements are necessary. |
| Track preference | Route introductory discussion; both tracks and uncertainty are valid. Review whether this must be required for membership. |
| Short interests answer | Support a relevant conversation. Review whether requiring it is necessary rather than merely useful. |
| Optional GitHub/LinkedIn | Voluntary context, not a prerequisite or permission for unrelated profile scraping. |
| Project interest | Identify interest in a separate activity. Review whether requiring it is necessary for membership; it must not become a project-eligibility test. |

Anonymous applications cannot support individual contact/admission. A short application is less intrusive than CVs, portfolios or extensive written evidence. Convenience alone does not establish necessity of all six required controls. Record field-level conclusions; if necessity cannot be supported, review the relevant field or basis through a separately approved change before launch. This task preserves all seven questions and their validation/required settings.

## Balancing test

Applicants initiating membership requests would reasonably expect Board review and contact, but not indefinite storage, public disclosure, automated ranking or unrelated scrutiny of profile histories. Students may feel pressure to disclose extensive experience; the existing copy instead emphasizes curiosity, brief answers and an optional profile.

Potential harms include exposure of contact details/free-text disclosures, unfair admission assumptions, prolonged storage and provider/transfer risks. The short form limits volume, not all risk. Do not request sensitive information or use unsolicited sensitive disclosures as selection criteria; establish handling guidance before launch.

Proposed safeguards: authorized Board access only; restricted editor/sheet sharing; hidden summaries; no forced Google identity collection, CV upload, automated scoring or acceptance; optional profile; clear bilingual notice/contact and objection handling; 90-day deletion of unsuccessful/withdrawn applications and accepted originals; minimum transfer to a separately governed register. Google arrangements, transfer information and deletion responsibilities remain unapproved. Closed-state checks verify settings, not compliance or future practice.

Provisional conclusion: a limited voluntary application could support legitimate interests if necessity is demonstrated and safeguards are implemented. There is insufficient evidence for an unconditional favorable conclusion while controller status, required-field necessity, provider arrangements and retention implementation remain under review.
